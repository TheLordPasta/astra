Mush Mush Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 23, 2026

Mush Mush is an AI-powered assistant designed to help fashion and textile businesses manage customer conversations, customer preferences, product information, and related business workflows.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how Mush Mush is used, the service may process the following information:

  • Instagram account information made available through Meta's Instagram APIs, such as account identifiers and usernames.
  • Instagram messages and message metadata when users communicate with an Instagram account connected to Mush Mush.
  • Customer information provided by a business, such as names, contact information, preferences, wishlist information, orders, and conversation history.
  • Product and business information entered into the Mush Mush system, including fabric information and business research data.
  • Images or other content submitted by users through supported integrations.

2. How We Use Information

We use information processed by Mush Mush to:

  • Receive and respond to customer messages on connected professional Instagram accounts.
  • Maintain customer conversation history and business records.
  • Store customer preferences and observations when requested or otherwise appropriate for the connected business workflow.
  • Search and use business information, including products, fabrics, designers, competitors, trends, orders, and customer data.
  • Analyze submitted images and text where the connected service supports those features.
  • Operate, maintain, secure, and improve the Mush Mush service.

3. Instagram Data

When Mush Mush is connected to an Instagram professional account, it accesses Instagram data only through Meta's authorized APIs and only for the permissions granted to the application.

Instagram data is used to provide the functionality requested by the connected business, including receiving and responding to messages and managing supported Instagram interactions.

4. Data Sharing

Mush Mush does not sell personal information.

Information may be processed by service providers that are necessary to operate the application, such as infrastructure, database, hosting, analytics, and AI service providers. Such providers receive information only as necessary to provide their services.

5. AI Processing

Mush Mush uses artificial intelligence to process text, images, customer conversations, and business information in order to provide assistant functionality.

Depending on the features being used, information may be sent to third-party AI infrastructure providers for processing on behalf of the application.

6. Data Retention

Information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the connected business service, maintain conversation history, support customer relationship management, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and maintain security.

Businesses using Mush Mush may request deletion of information associated with their customers, subject to applicable legal, technical, and contractual requirements.

7. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures intended to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

No method of storing or transmitting information over the Internet can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. User Rights and Requests

Depending on applicable law, individuals may have rights relating to their personal information, including rights to access, correction, deletion, restriction, or objection to certain processing activities.

Requests regarding personal information processed by a specific business should generally be directed to that business first.

9. Children's Privacy

Mush Mush is intended for business use and is not designed for children under the age required by applicable law.

10. Third-Party Services

Mush Mush may interact with third-party platforms and services, including Meta's Instagram platform and AI service providers. Those services may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

11. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes to the Mush Mush service, legal requirements, or the technologies used by the application.

The effective date shown at the top of this page indicates when this version of the policy became effective.

12. Contact

For privacy-related questions or requests concerning Mush Mush, please contact the operator of the Mush Mush application or the business that connected the application to its account.
